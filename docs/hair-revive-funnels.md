# Hair Revive campaign family

`hair-revive-ar` and `hair-revive-en` are independent native funnel templates. Their fifteen editable sections use eleven section types, with five statement bands. The composition includes a photographic hero, numbered product details, optional video, alternating image comparisons, information cards, editorial quote, marquee, actual product reviews, FAQ and footer. A fixed purchase dock shares the selected variant's price and image with `FunnelCheckout`.

The public gallery preview is explicitly synthetic and cannot initialize checkout, load a real product or submit an order. Its sample reviews are identified individually. Real funnels render approved API reviews only; empty comparisons, information cards, quotes and marquee content start hidden until the merchant supplies them. Campaign defaults never invent clinical outcomes, endorsements, sales figures or a resetting countdown.

Typography uses locally hosted Alexandria, DM Sans and Readex Pro with their licenses. The desktop content container is capped at 1536px. The mobile navigation uses a 250px native modal drawer with Escape and focus restoration. FAQ answers are single-open. Reveal animation respects reduced motion; YouTube embeds load only after interaction and use the privacy domain. Customizer previews have disabled checkout.

Original generated images and exact prompts are documented in `public/media/funnel-campaigns/hair-revive-SOURCE.txt`. Gallery thumbnails are screenshots of the native implementation. No competitor assets, customer identities, endorsements or medical claims are copied into merchant defaults.
